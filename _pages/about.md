I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Neural Computation and Control Laboratory, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), starting July 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Information and Systems Engineering from Concordia University (2025), and was a Postdoctoral Associate at the Cumming School of Medicine, University of Calgary (Nov. 2025 – Apr. 2026), working on predictive modeling with real-world healthcare data.

My research lies at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience, artificial intelligence, and NeuroAI. The core question that drives me is: *how do humans rapidly adapt to new environments from limited experience, and compose, reuse, and generalize what they have learned?* Drawing inspiration from human perception and cognition, I aim to design machine intelligence systems that can keep learning and evolving in open-ended environments.

As a first step, I proposed non-parametric hierarchical clustering algorithms inspired by human visual perception. Based on Gestalt principles, the algorithms quantify proximity and continuity as adaptive merging criteria, so that structure is built bottom-up from local cues. They address long-standing challenges in clustering: determining the number of clusters, handling poorly-separated data, and adapting to arbitrary-shape distributions. This work has been published in IEEE TPAMI, Pattern Recognition, and Pattern Recognition Letters.
